Mission Architecture

Precision before altitude.

Every build is organized around subsystem ownership: propulsion, structures, avionics, recovery, payload, and operations. The goal is not just to fly. The goal is to understand why it flew.

01Structures

Airframe geometry, fin alignment, strength, and fit.

02Avionics

Flight computers, logging, sensors, and validation.

03Recovery

Deployment timing, tracking, and safe return.

04Payload

Experiment integration, isolation, and access.
